----------------------
Material Data Configs.
----------------------
BEGIN INSTRUCTIONS
1) Read the variable descriptions below. They are material properties:
	rho: 		density, 				kg/m^3
	cp: 		specific heat at constant pressure, 	J/(kg-K)
	T_melt: 	melting temperature, 			K
	E:		elastic modulus, 			Pa
	CTE:		coefficient of thermal expansion, 	K^-1
	epsilon:	surface roughness, 			m
	k:		thermal conductivity, 			W/(m-K)

2) To add a new material config., copy the format below

\EXAMPLE\
rho = <value>
cp = <value>
T_melt = <value>
E = <value>
CTE = <value>
epsilon = <value>
k = <value>
\end\

3) Separate material configs. as shown below

\material_1\
<properties>
\end\

\material_2\
<properties>
\end\

END INSTRUCTIONS
